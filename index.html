07 / Questions

Is it flat pack or assembled?+

Cabinetry is supplied flat pack, cut to your dimensions, with hardware and assembly instructions included.

Who installs it?+

You do, your builder does, or your cabinetmaker does. We supply full setout drawings so any competent installer can fit it. We are a supplier, not a building contractor.

What does a kitchen cost?+

Signature kitchenettes run from $4,590 to $7,500, priced on this page. Custom layouts are priced live in the planner, and the figure is fixed in writing before anything is manufactured.

Do you deliver outside Queensland?+

Flat pack ships, so yes — by arrangement. Brisbane, the Sunshine Coast and Rockhampton are our regular runs.

What does the warranty cover?+

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Full warranty terms are confirmed in writing with your quote.
