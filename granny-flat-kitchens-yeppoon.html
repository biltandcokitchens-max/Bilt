Livingstone Shire, not Rockhampton

This is the one that trips people up. Yeppoon, Cooee Bay, Lammermoor, Taranganba, Barmaryee, Zilzie and Emu Park are all Livingstone Shire Council, with their own planning scheme. Whether a secondary dwelling is permitted on your block, how big it can be, and whether it can be tenanted separately are all their decisions, not ours and not Rockhampton’s. Ask them before you spend anything — we cover what the classification means for the kitchen in our Class 1a guide.

Salt air is a specification problem

Coastal humidity and salt do two things to cabinetry: they get into unsealed board edges, and they corrode cheap hardware. Neither shows up in year one. We specify moisture-resistant carcasses and laser-bonded edging as standard, which matters more here than fifty kilometres inland, and we would not use unbranded runners on a coastal job at any price.

Built for whoever lives there next

Secondary dwellings on this coast get used three ways — family, long-term tenant, or holiday letting — and they often change between them. Specify for the hardest of those and you never have to think about it again. If holiday letting is the plan, the brief changes again: see short-stay kitchens on the Capricorn Coast.

Which council covers granny flats in Yeppoon?

Livingstone Shire Council, not Rockhampton Regional Council. Their planning scheme decides whether a secondary dwelling is permitted on your block and on what conditions. Confirm with them before committing to a design.

Does a granny flat in Yeppoon need a full kitchen?

If it is approved as a dwelling, it generally needs the facilities of one — a sink, a cooking facility and a bench. Your building certifier confirms the requirement against the approved classification.

How much is a granny flat kitchen in Yeppoon?

From about $6,500 for a 2.4 metre run and $7,700 for 3.0 metres, including carcasses, doors, Blum hardware and a benchtop. Yeppoon is inside our standard service area with no travel loading.

Do you install in Yeppoon yourself?

Yes. Our own employed team, forty minutes from Rockhampton. We do not subcontract Capricorn Coast work.
