Do you ship flat pack kitchens anywhere in NSW?

Yes — Sydney, Newcastle and Wollongong have their own pages, and we ship on the same terms to any NSW postcode. Send your address with the quote request and freight is confirmed directly.

Is a flat pack kitchen cheaper in regional NSW than Sydney?

The cabinetry price is the same everywhere; only the freight line changes with distance and postcode, and it is quoted separately rather than folded into the price.

Do you install kitchens in NSW?

No — our installation team works Central Queensland only. Everywhere in NSW is supply: flat pack or delivered assembled, fitted by your own builder or installer.

What is the difference between this page and the Sydney page?

This page covers the whole state; the Sydney page has content specific to that city. Both offer the same kitchen on the same terms — use whichever is more relevant to you.
