## The core finding

The site currently owns **Stage 2 → Stage 0** — design, pricing, purchase. Everything
upstream is empty. A grep for flood, insurance, NDIS, accessible, aging in place,
wheelchair, grant, equity, subdivide, elderly parent, downsizing returns **nothing**.

That is the entire top half of the funnel, and it is where the competition is weakest,
because kitchen companies write about kitchens.

## Constraints carried over

- Regulation pages: no fee, setback, timeframe or clause stated unless sourced and
  linked; direct council link on every page; "last checked" date; describe process
  rather than quoting figures that go stale.
- NDIS and insurance pages describe *what is typically covered and who to ask* — not
  eligibility determinations.
- Every new keyword gets a single `owner_page` in `KEYWORD-MAP.csv` so this expansion
  cannot create cannibalisation.
- Maintenance: regulation and funding pages need a twice-yearly review.
