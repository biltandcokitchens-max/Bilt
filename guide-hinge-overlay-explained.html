Full, half and inset:
hinge overlay explained.

Overlay describes how much of the cabinet side a door covers. It sounds like a detail and it decides how the finished kitchen looks, because it sets the gaps between doors.

The short answer

Overlay is how far a door sits over the cabinet side next to it. Full overlay covers the whole side, which is what gives a modern kitchen its continuous run of doors with narrow even gaps. Half overlay covers half, used where two doors share a middle panel. Inset sits the door flush inside the frame, which is a traditional look and the least forgiving of any movement in the cabinet.

Full overlay

The standard for contemporary kitchens. The door covers the cabinet side almost entirely, and adjacent doors are separated by a gap of a few millimetres. It hides the carcass completely, which means the carcass material matters less visually — and it makes even gaps essential, because a run of full-overlay doors shows any misalignment immediately.

Half overlay

Used where two cabinets share a side and each door covers half of it. You see it in older kitchens and in some flat pack systems. It is not a worse choice, but it produces a wider line between doors, which reads as more traditional.

Inset

The door sits inside the cabinet opening, flush with the frame. It is the hardest to execute because the gap around the door is visible on all four sides, so any movement in the carcass or the house shows. It suits Shaker and period kitchens and it demands a carcass that stays square.

Ask us directly →
What does full overlay mean?

The door covers almost the whole cabinet side, leaving only a few millimetres of gap to the next door. It is the standard for modern kitchens.

What is the difference between overlay and inset?

Overlay doors sit on top of the cabinet frame. Inset doors sit inside it, flush with the front. Inset is a traditional look and shows any misalignment on all four sides.

Which overlay should I choose?

Full overlay for a contemporary kitchen, which is what most people want. Inset if you are after a period or Shaker look and accept the tighter tolerances.

Can hinges be adjusted after fitting?

Blum hinges adjust in three directions, so doors can be brought back into line without removing anything. That is most of why we specify them.
