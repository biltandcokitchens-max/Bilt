Standard kitchen
cabinet sizes.

There is no law setting kitchen cabinet sizes in Australia. There is a convention, and it exists because of appliance sizes and sheet material widths rather than because anyone legislated it.

The short answer

Australian base cabinets are typically 720mm high before the kickboard, giving a finished bench height near 900mm, and typically 560 to 600mm deep. Overheads are usually 300 to 350mm deep. Widths run in 150mm steps from 300mm up to about 1200mm. Those are conventions driven by appliance sizes and by the width of the board the cabinets are cut from, not rules — and a cabinet cut to your wall will always fit better than one picked from the list.

Where the numbers come from

A bench near 900mm suits the height of most adults working standing up, and it is the height dishwashers and under-bench ovens are built to slide under. A base depth of 560 to 600mm matches the depth of those same appliances and leaves the benchtop a small overhang at the front. Overheads are shallower so you can stand at the bench without hitting your head. None of it is arbitrary, and none of it is compulsory.

Why widths go in steps

Catalogue cabinets come in fixed widths because a factory making thousands of the same box is cheaper than one making yours. The steps are usually 150mm. The consequence is that a 3140mm wall gets cabinets adding to 3000mm and a 140mm filler panel doing the rest, and that panel is dead space you paid for.

When the convention is worth breaking

Tall people often want a bench above 900mm, and it costs nothing to specify at the drawing stage. A wheelchair user needs a lower working height, sometimes two heights in one kitchen. A galley under 3m wants shallower base cabinets so the walkway still works. An old house with a 2.4m ceiling wants different overhead heights from a new one with 2.7m.

What is the standard kitchen bench height in Australia?

Around 900mm finished, which comes from a 720mm base cabinet plus a kickboard and the benchtop. It is a convention rather than a rule, and it is worth changing if the people using the kitchen are notably tall or short.

How deep are kitchen cabinets?

Base cabinets are typically 560 to 600mm deep and overheads 300 to 350mm. The base depth is set by the appliances that have to fit under the bench.

What widths do kitchen cabinets come in?

Catalogue cabinets usually step in 150mm increments from 300mm to about 1200mm. Cut-to-size cabinetry has no steps, which is how a run fills a wall without filler panels.

Can I get non-standard cabinet sizes?

Yes. Everything we cut is drawn to your room, so the sizes follow the wall rather than a list.
