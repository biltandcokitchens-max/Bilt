Trades and approvals

What is different
about Tasmania.

The cabinetry is identical in every state. What changes is who licenses the trades who connect it and who assesses a second dwelling if you are building one. In Tasmania, plumbing and electrical licences are checked with Consumer, Building and Occupational Services. Ask for the number, look it up yourself, and ask for evidence of insurance at the same time — it takes a minute and an unlicensed connection can fail an inspection, void an insurance claim and surface in a building report when you sell.

If the kitchen is going into a granny flat, studio or secondary dwelling, the planning rules are set by your council under the state framework and they change. Granny flat rules in Tasmania covers what to check before you design anything.

Ask us directly →
Do you deliver flat pack kitchens to Tasmania?

Yes, to every postcode. Kitchens are cut to your drawing in Rockhampton and freighted across, flat packed or delivered assembled, with freight quoted to your postcode as its own line.

Do you install kitchens in Tasmania?

No. Our own team installs in Central Queensland only. In Tasmania your builder, carpenter or kitchen installer fits it from the dimensioned service drawings that ship with the order.

Who do I check a tradesperson's licence with in Tasmania?

Consumer, Building and Occupational Services. Ask for the licence number and look it up yourself before anyone starts, and ask for evidence of insurance at the same time.

Can I order without visiting a showroom?

That is the only way we work. Send the room dimensions and photos, a designer draws it, and you get the drawing and a fixed itemised quote back. Nothing is due to see either, and nothing is cut until you sign the drawing off.
