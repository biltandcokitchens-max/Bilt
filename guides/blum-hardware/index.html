Blum hardware,
and why it is standard.

If you compare one line between kitchen quotes, make it this one.

Design your kitchen See pricing

The cabinetry outlives the hardware, every time

Pull apart a twenty-year-old kitchen and the carcasses are usually sound. What has failed is the moving parts: hinges that have dropped so the doors no longer line up, runners that grind or tip, drawers that have to be lifted to close.

That is the normal failure pattern, and it is why hardware brand tells you more about how long a kitchen will last than the door finish does.

It is also, conveniently, the least visible line on a quote. A cabinet price with unbranded hardware inside it looks identical to one with good hardware until about year five.

What is actually included here

Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION hinges. Soft-close is built into the hinge rather than added as a separate damper. They clip on and off without tools, which matters the first time you need to take a door off to get an appliance through.

Blum TANDEMBOX antaro runners. The load is carried on the runner rather than on the drawer base, which is what keeps a full drawer gliding instead of dragging and sagging. Also soft-close as standard.

Both are on every cabinet. Not an upgrade tier, not a tick-box, and not something you can accidentally leave off the order.

What to ask anyone else

Three questions, and they take a minute:

What brand of hardware is included at this price? A good cabinetmaker answers immediately and is usually pleased to be asked. Vagueness is itself an answer.

Is soft-close included or extra? In several flat-pack systems the hinges or drawer slides are purchased separately from the cabinet, so the quoted cabinet price may not include the thing that decides how the kitchen feels.

What is the warranty on the hardware specifically? Cabinet warranty and hardware warranty are frequently different, and the hardware is what fails.

Questions

Hardware, specifically.

Is Blum hardware worth paying extra for?

You are not paying extra for it here — it is standard on every cabinet. As a general question: hinges and runners are what fail first in a kitchen, so it is the component where quality has the most effect on how long the kitchen stays pleasant to use.

What is CLIP top BLUMOTION?

A Blum hinge with soft-close built into the hinge itself rather than added as a separate damper, and which clips on and off without tools.

What is TANDEMBOX antaro?

A Blum drawer runner system that carries the load on the runner rather than the drawer base. That is what stops a heavily loaded drawer from dragging or sagging.

Do I have to choose soft-close?

No — it is standard on every hinge and every runner. There is nothing to select and nothing to forget.
