What should a McLaren Vale kitchen be built to survive?

Farm houses take a kind of use that suburban kitchens do not — boots, buckets, big cooking, and a back door that is really the front door. The specification we ship assumes that rather than hoping otherwise. A lot of 1960s and 70s houses on generous blocks, which is why second dwellings and garage conversions come up so often here.

What does delivery to McLaren Vale involve?

Your kitchen is cut in Rockhampton and freighted west via the Barrier and Sturt highways. Flat cartons are labelled to a numbered drawing, one per cabinet where possible, with doors and fronts wrapped separately and the Blum hardware bagged against the cabinet it belongs to. Assembled orders arrive as finished boxes, doors hung and adjusted, which takes more truck space and shows as a higher freight line. Both are on the same quote so the choice is a number rather than a guess.

Who fits it in McLaren Vale?

Your builder, carpenter or kitchen installer, plus a licensed plumber and a licensed electrician for their parts. Ask each for a licence number and check it yourself — in South Australia that is SA Consumer and Business Services. Every order ships with dimensioned service drawings showing each waste, water point and outlet, so the trades rough in to the plan rather than to a guess. Our install guide sets out the three trades and the fixed order they work in.

How do I compare this with a McLaren Vale cabinetmaker?

Take our itemised quote to any McLaren Vale cabinetmaker and compare it line for line, which is exactly what it is formatted for. Ask them three things: board thickness and moisture rating, edging method, hardware brand. If the answers match ours and the number is better, take theirs - a local supplier with the same specification and no freight line is a genuinely good outcome. If the answers are vague, you have learned something either way.

What should be ready before the kitchen lands?

The old kitchen out, the floor as level as it is going to get, walls patched and painted where the cabinets will not cover them, and the plumber and electrician booked for the days after the fit rather than the same day. Painting after the cabinetry is in is slower and the cut-in never looks as good. Our installation guide sets out the order of trades.

What happens if something arrives damaged?

Check the delivery against the numbered drawing on the day it lands in McLaren Vale, before the truck leaves if you can. Count the cartons, look at the door and drawer fronts. If a panel is damaged or a carton is missing, photograph it and ring the studio the same day. A replacement is cut from the drawing we hold on file, so it matches the rest of the kitchen rather than being re-measured from scratch.

Where else do you ship in Greater Adelaide?

We ship to Gawler, Mount Barker, Victor Harbor, Aldinga, Elizabeth, Salisbury, Glenelg, Noarlunga and the rest of the region on the same route. The Greater Adelaide page covers the region, and South Australia covers the state.

Do you deliver flat pack kitchens to McLaren Vale?

Yes. McLaren Vale is served west via the Barrier and Sturt highways, and freight is quoted to your postcode as its own line on the quote rather than folded into the cabinetry price.

Do you install kitchens in McLaren Vale?

No. Our own installation team works Central Queensland only. In McLaren Vale your builder, carpenter or kitchen installer fits it, working from the dimensioned service drawings that ship with every order. Our install guide covers the three trades and the order they work in.

Should I order flat pack or assembled to McLaren Vale?

Both are quoted on the same drawing. Flat cartons ship for less and get through tight doorways; assembled arrives with carcasses built and doors adjusted so your installer is fitting rather than building. Over this distance most Greater Adelaide orders go flat.

Who can do the plumbing and electrical in McLaren Vale?

A licensed plumber and a licensed electrician, and you should check both licences yourself before they start. In South Australia, SA Consumer and Business Services is the register to check against.

What board and hardware do you use?

18mm moisture-resistant board, laser-bonded edging and soft-close hinges and full-extension runners on every cabinet, in every state, with the hardware brand named on your quote. The specification is printed on your quote so you can hold it against anything else you are offered.

Is there a deposit to get a McLaren Vale quote?

No. The drawing and the itemised quote cost nothing and carry no obligation. Payment terms for the order itself are stated on that quote.

Can I add pull-downs and organisers to a McLaren Vale kitchen?

Yes, and they are quoted per item rather than bundled into a package you did not ask for. Pull-down wall baskets, magic corners, tall larder pull-outs and drawer organisers can all go into a McLaren Vale order. If you order delivered assembled they are fitted before it ships; flat packed, they arrive with the cabinet they belong to.
