Layouts

Kitchen layouts,
and what each one needs.

The layout decides how the kitchen feels to use far more than the finish does.

Design your kitchen See pricing

Most kitchen advice starts with colour. It should start with the shape of the room, because that is what determines whether two people can work in it at once and whether the door of the dishwasher blocks the fridge.

Layouts

Choose a layout.

  • Galley kitchen

    Two parallel runs — the most efficient layout there is, and the least forgiving of width.

  • L-shaped kitchen

    Two runs meeting at a corner — the most common layout in Australia, and the corner is the catch.

  • U-shaped kitchen

    Three runs, maximum storage, two corners to solve.

  • Island kitchen

    The one everybody wants — and the one most often squeezed into a room that cannot take it.
