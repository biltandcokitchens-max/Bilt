Do you build kitchens in Yeppoon?

Yes. Yeppoon is inside Bilt & Co's standard service area with no travel loading — we are based 40 minutes away in Rockhampton. We site measure in Yeppoon weekly.

How much does a kitchen cost in Yeppoon?

The same as Rockhampton: $15,000 to $23,000 for our Essence collection, $26,000 to $42,000 for Maison, and $47,000 and up for fully bespoke Atelier work. We do not charge a premium for Yeppoon projects.

Will your own team install it?

Yes. Our employed installation team works across the region every week — we do not subcontract Yeppoon jobs to a third party. Same crew, same ten-year warranty.

Is the design consultation really free in Yeppoon?

Yes — the site measure, the 3D design and the fixed quote are free anywhere in our service area, Yeppoon included. If our number does not work for you, you keep the drawings.
